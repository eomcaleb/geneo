01 Humanity createdGenesis 1 · the root of every line day 6 ›

Humankind (adam) is made in God’s image, male and female, and told to multiply. The genealogy starts here — with a blessing to be fruitful.

1:1God creates the heavens and the earth. 1:26“Let us make man in our image” — adam as a kind, not yet a name. 1:27Created male and female. 1:28“Be fruitful and multiply” — the first mandate for descendants. 1:31Day six. Everything is “very good.”
02 The first coupleGenesis 2 · Adam and the woman 2 people ›

The lens zooms from humanity to one man and one woman. The first toledot (“these are the generations”) heading appears, and marriage is set as the frame for every family that follows.

2:4Toledot #1 — “generations of the heavens and the earth.” 2:7The man is formed from the dust and given the breath of life. 2:8Placed in Eden. 2:22The woman is made from his rib. 2:24A man leaves his parents and holds fast to his wife — the template for every household after.
03 The fall and the promised seedGenesis 3 · Eve named 1 promise ›

Nobody is born yet, but the line gets its purpose: a coming “seed” of the woman. Childbirth now comes with pain, and death enters the story. The woman gets her name.

3:6The man and woman eat from the forbidden tree. 3:15Seed of the woman vs seed of the serpent — the thread every later genealogy follows. 3:16Pain in childbearing. 3:19“To dust you shall return” — death enters, so lifespans start to count. 3:20Named Eve, “mother of all living.” 3:24Sent out of Eden.
04 Two lines divergeGenesis 4 · Cain, Abel, Seth 17 names ›

The first children are born, and the first one kills the second. The family splits: Cain’s line builds cities, invents crafts and escalates violence, while Seth’s line begins to “call on the name of the LORD.”

05 Book of the generations of AdamGenesis 5 · Adam → Noah, with ages 10 gens ›

The first formal genealogy (toledot #2, 5:1). Each entry follows a set pattern: lived X years, fathered Y, lived Z more, died. The one break is Enoch, who doesn’t die. The ages give a full timeline from Adam to the Flood.

Lifespans · years after creation (AM)

Bar = lifetime. Notch = age at fathering the named son. │ = Flood (AM 1656). ↑ = taken. Tap a name to see who was alive at the same time.
